## What Drove the Q3 Improvement

Q3's $330,000 total marketing spend (see Finance's `quarterly_financial_report.md` for the full breakdown) funded three initiatives:

1. **Nationwide re-branding initiative** ($21,000 incremental) — refreshed visual identity ahead of Q4 renewal season, contributing to a measurable lift in landing-page conversion (3.1% → 4.4%) attributed in part to improved trust signals in the new brand design.
2. **Enterprise fintech sponsorship** ($15,000 incremental) — sponsorship of a mid-sized industry conference generated 340+ qualified enterprise leads, of which 58 converted to paid accounts by end of Q3 (a 17% lead-to-customer rate, well above the 9% blended average).
3. **5-week paid acquisition surge** ($9,000 incremental, plus reallocated base ad spend) — launched in direct response to a competitor's pricing change; drove the bulk of the 48.3% new-customer growth, though at a slightly lower average LTV per acquired customer than organic channels ($912 vs. $1,020 blended).
