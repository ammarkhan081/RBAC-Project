Campaign ROI averaged 356% across the year, with Q3's 410% as the annual high, before settling to 372% in Q4 as acquisition spend normalized post-rebrand.

## Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) & Lifetime Value (LTV) — Definitions

- **CAC** = Total acquisition-attributed marketing spend for the period ÷ New customers acquired in that period.
- **LTV** = Average revenue per customer over a trailing 24-month window, adjusted for churn observed in the cohort.
- **Campaign ROI** = (Revenue attributed to campaigns − Ad spend) ÷ Ad spend, 90-day attribution window.
