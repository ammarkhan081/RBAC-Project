# FinSolve Technologies — Employee Handbook

*Access: All employees (General role and above). This document contains no confidential or role-restricted information.*

## 1. Leave Entitlements

| Leave Type | Entitlement (per year) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Casual Leave | 10 days | Can be taken in single days; no carry-forward |
| Sick Leave | 8 days | Requires manager notification within 24 hours |
| Annual/Vacation Leave | 18 days | Up to 5 days carry-forward into the next year |
| Maternity Leave | 90 calendar days (paid) | As per Pakistan labor law minimums; extendable unpaid by mutual agreement |
| Paternity Leave | 10 working days (paid) | To be taken within 3 months of the child's birth |
| Public Holidays | 14 days/year | Per the national holiday calendar, published annually by HR |
| Bereavement Leave | 5 days | Immediate family; documentation may be requested |

## 2. Compensation Rules (General Policy)

- Salaries are reviewed annually, with performance-linked adjustments effective each January.
- Overtime for non-exempt roles is compensated at 1.5x the standard hourly rate.
- Referral bonuses: PKR 50,000 equivalent for a successful hire who completes a 90-day probation period, paid in the following payroll cycle.
- Compensation bands and individual salary figures are HR/Executive-restricted information and are not published in this handbook.

## 3. Attendance & Working Hours

- Standard working hours: 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM, Monday–Friday, with a 1-hour lunch break.
- Remote and hybrid arrangements are approved on a role-by-role basis by direct managers.
- Attendance is tracked via the internal HR system; persistent attendance issues are addressed through the standard performance-management process, not through this handbook.

## 4. Data Security Guidelines (All Employees)

1. **Least privilege by default.** Only request access to systems and data required for your specific role. Access requests outside your role require manager and data-owner approval.
2. **No credential sharing.** Login credentials, API keys, and access tokens are individual and must never be shared, including with colleagues in the same department.
3. **Sensitive data handling.** Financial records, HR/payroll data, and customer PII must never be exported to personal devices, personal email, or unapproved third-party tools (including public AI chatbots).
4. **Report suspected incidents immediately.** Any suspected data leak, phishing attempt, or unauthorized access should be reported to the Security/IT team within the same business day.
5. **Internal AI assistant usage.** The company's internal AI assistant enforces role-based access automatically — if it declines to answer a question, this reflects an access boundary, not a system error. Do not attempt to rephrase queries to bypass a declined request; report it to IT if you believe your role should have access.
6. **Device security.** Company devices must have disk encryption and auto-lock enabled. Loss or theft of a company device must be reported within 24 hours.

## 5. Code of Conduct (Summary)

FinSolve Technologies is committed to a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free workplace. Full policy details, reporting channels, and escalation paths are maintained by HR and are available on request. This handbook provides a summary only; the complete Code of Conduct document supersedes this summary in case of any discrepancy.

## 6. Questions?

For questions about this handbook, leave balances, or company policy, contact HR directly. This document — along with company FAQs — is available to every employee through the internal assistant under the "General" access role, with no cross-departmental data exposed.
