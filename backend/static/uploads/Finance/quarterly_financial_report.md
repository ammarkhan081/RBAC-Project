## Q3 2024 — Operating Expense Breakdown

| Category | Amount | % of OpEx |
|---|---|---|
| Salaries & Benefits | $1,215,000 | 48.5% |
| Vendor & Third-Party Services | $390,000 | 15.6% |

| Infrastructure & Cloud Costs | $340,000 | 13.6% |

| **Total** | **$2,505,000** | 100% |

**Why Q3 marketing expense rose by ~$45,000 quarter-over-quarter (from $285,000 to $330,000):** three concurrent initiatives drove the increase —

1. **Nationwide re-branding initiative** — new brand identity rollout across web, app, and print collateral, timed ahead of Q4 renewal season ($21,000 of the increase).
2. **Enterprise fintech sponsorship** — FinSolve sponsored a mid-sized industry conference to build enterprise-segment credibility, generating 340+ qualified leads ($15,000 of the increase).
3. **Customer acquisition surge** — a short-term paid acquisition push in response to a competitor's pricing change, run for 5 weeks in August–September ($9,000 of the increase).

Gross margin dipped slightly to 60.4% this quarter due to the elevated infrastructure spend (a planned database migration) landing in the same quarter as the marketing increase — both are one-off/planned costs, not a trend.
